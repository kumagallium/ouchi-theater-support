Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-30

Scope

This policy applies to the current version of Ouchi Theater.

Photo library access

With your permission, the app reads videos, audio, thumbnails, album names, recording dates, and related information to browse, filter, and play your videos. The app does not edit or delete your original photos or videos.

On-device processing and storage

Audio from videos already retrieved for playback is analyzed on your device to adjust differences in volume. Sort and date-filter settings, loudness measurements, video identifiers, modification dates, and local file locations are stored on your device. Audio is not saved separately or sent to the developer for loudness analysis.

Network access and external services

The app has no feature that sends videos, audio, or photo-library information to a developer-operated server. It includes no advertising, usage analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. Videos and thumbnails in iCloud Photos or shared albums may be downloaded using Apple photo-library services. Data charges may apply. Apple processes data according to its own policies.

Diagnostics and support

Choosing Copy in the diagnostics screen places information such as video identifiers, file names, dates, formats, and loading errors on the clipboard. This information is not sent automatically. Remove personal or unnecessary information before sharing it with support. Email and attachments you choose to send are used to answer your request and investigate problems; information no longer needed for support is deleted.

Permissions and deletion

You can change or revoke photo-library access in device settings. Deleting the app removes its local settings and analysis records, while original videos remain in Photos. Offloading the app retains its data. Settings may be restored from a device backup.

Website and email services

The policy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. The developer does not add website analytics or advertising cookies. Support email is received through Gmail. Processing by these services is subject to the GitHub and Google privacy policies. Reading the policy bundled inside the app does not connect to the website.

Trial and purchase verification

In purchase-enabled versions, the free trial and one-time unlock use Apple in-app purchases. The app receives verified transaction details from Apple, including products, original purchase dates, and revocation status, to determine access and the 30-day period on your device. The developer does not receive your payment details. The last observed time is stored locally to reduce clock rollback. Transactions are not sent to a developer-operated server. Purchases and restoration communicate with Apple.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The revision date is shown on this page.
