Help and Support

Updated: 2026-10-30

Videos are missing

Check photo-library access for Ouchi Theater in Settings. If access is limited, add the videos you want to use. For shared albums, also check the Shared Albums setting for Photos.

Loading takes a long time

Videos stored in iCloud may take time to download. Check your internet connection and available storage, and try playing the video in Photos.

Selecting videos

Tap videos or drag sideways to select several. With no selection, all videos currently shown will play. In the date calendar, drag across dates to select a range and drag either end to adjust it.

Supported devices

Requires an iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. Japanese and English are supported. Operation on every device and external-display configuration is not guaranteed.

Contacting support

Include your device model, OS and app version, and the steps that caused the issue. There is no need to send family videos or personal information. Review diagnostics before sharing them.
